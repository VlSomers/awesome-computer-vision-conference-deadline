- [Acceptance rate](https://github.com/lixin4ever/Conference-Acceptance-Rate)

### How to read the tables
- **Paper Registration** is the *earliest mandatory* deadline of the main track: the one after which a new paper can no longer be submitted (paper registration, abstract submission, or the paper deadline itself when there is no earlier step). The full-paper deadline usually follows a few days to a week later. Dates are the final ones (after extensions) and are mostly Anywhere on Earth (AoE): check the official call for papers for the exact time.
- **WACV** has two submission rounds: `R1` / `R2` give the earliest mandatory deadline of each round.
- **Event Start-End Dates** cover the whole event, workshops and tutorials included.
- **TBA** means not announced yet.
